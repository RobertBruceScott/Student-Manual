Second Edition

The forthcoming second edition of A Student's Manual for A First Course in General Relativity accompanies the third edition of Bernard Schutz's A First Course in General Relativity.

The new edition provides step-by-step guidance through more than 200 selected exercises, with detailed solutions and explanatory comments cross-referenced to the relevant equations and sections in Schutz's text. It also includes 168 supplementary problems designed to highlight conceptual challenges and direct readers to useful supporting literature.

The book includes a comprehensive index with bolded keywords, as well as an appendix of useful mathematical and physical results, making it useful both as a study guide and as a lasting reference for advanced undergraduate students, graduate students, instructors, and self-directed learners.

What's new in the second edition?

  • Revised to accompany the third edition of Schutz's textbook.
  • New material supporting Schutz's chapter on gravitational wave astronomy.
  • Substantial updates to the later applications chapters.
  • Solutions to about half of the new exercises in Schutz's third edition.
  • New hints and supplementary problems in most chapters.
  • Improvements to many of the solutions in the earlier chapters.
  • A new Mathematica notebook replacing the older Maple worksheet.
  • Use of boldface keywords to help readers scan for indexed terms.
  • Correction of terminology from “Lorentz gauge” to “Lorenz gauge.”

About the Author

Robert B. Scott teaches in the physics department at the Université de Bretagne Occidentale, where he specializes in general relativity, special relativity, and cosmology. He previously held postdoctoral research positions at University College London and Princeton University before becoming a research scientist at the University of Texas at Austin.

His research includes geophysical fluid dynamics, ocean turbulence, the visualization of curved spacetimes, and biophysics.
